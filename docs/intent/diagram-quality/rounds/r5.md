**Script change this round (step 1 of the round).** `edge-through-title-band` no longer counts
the crossing decision 3 allows: a line to or from a member that crosses the whole band straight
across, clear of the title text. A line along a band, through the title, or to something outside
the group still counts. The title's extent comes from the rendered SVG (icon, then the 13px title
at `x + 33`). Under main's script, round 5's code had five such hits: vn-architecture e2's
query / action through SolidStart's and Clients' bands, web-app's useSession → ProfileCard through
AppLayout's and Sidebar's, and incident-response's war room → status page through
Communications'. All five pass beside the title, and the new rule removes all five. Round 4's code
had none, so the baseline doesn't move.

- **A line along a box's own border** (0px), group-packing / routing-clearance: scan v0 (S1), and
  file-session e2's Save handler wrap (partly). Extend `edge-hugs-node` to d ≥ 0, or count a run
  on a side as `edge-through-node`.

## What changed, and which product decisions cost too much

1. **Returns blocked by a label take the longer side, else the target's side face (#82): kept, no
   cost seen.** srcly-release's S1 is gone; the C-shaped arrival reads as drafted.
2. **Chains of groups wrap into rows; 5:1 cap (#78): kept at the root, costly inside groups.**
   Root wraps (tasks, order-pipeline, hn-offline's sink seat) read well. Folding a group in place
   (file-session e2, scan v0, ufw e1) makes the group's lines double back, leaves its right half
   empty, and in scan v0 draws a line on three boxes' sides (S1). Prefer wrapping the root, or
   folding a group only when its run is a straight chain.
3. **Title-band crossing straight into a member above a 1.75× detour (#71): kept.** It removed the
   two worst loops (vn-architecture e2, web-app) and long-edge fell 24 → 14 with #78. No crossing
   seen runs through a title.
4. **Relabels promise `still-shape`, not `still *` (#74): kept.** All three corpus relabels and the
   new kroger e2 pass `still-shape`. add-metric e2 still fails `keeps-order` (10 flips across rows),
   which is a real signal.
5. **Growth drops stale stays (#76): kept.** hn-offline e1/e2 are the round's biggest single gains
   (13.8 → 6.2, 18.7 → 8.7). No stay that still fits was dropped.
6. **Channels: drop-and-repack deferred (#72).** uneven-padding 4 → 5 and padding-varies 3 → 3
   stay. The waste is S3 (file-session, mcp-call, tasks); #72's 2× compile time is not worth it
   yet.
7. **Diamonds take one edge per vertex, extras on side vertices (#79): kept for two exits, costly
   for three.** off-axis-port 40 → 12. But a third exit takes whichever vertex is free even when
   its target lies the other way: credit e2's `expired or failed` loops under the diamond through
   the `no` label (S1), and reading-flow e1's `no` loops over the drawing. A free vertex facing
   away from the target should lose to sharing the vertex that faces it.

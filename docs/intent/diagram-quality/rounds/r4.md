## False alarms and metric notes

- **Known false alarms:** `left-of build runtime` (hn-deploy, fold) and `above …` on a stress
  layout (code-layers) remain. So do 1px and 9px `near` misses (decisory e1 121px, add-metric
  e1 121px).
- **`hook` counts C-shaped returns.** It counts a line into a perpendicular side through a
  channel the same as a real U-turn, and over-counts as a result:
  - multi-region's Queue → Workers and kubernetes' web-7f9c → cart run down a channel into the
    side that faces it. Those read as drafted.
  - API → Queue entering Queue from below is a real hook.
  - Counting these would need to tell a U into the side the line left from a C into a facing
    side.
- **`long-edge` moves with the median.** Tight packing shortens most edges, so lines that kept
  their length now cross 4 × the median: ingest's skip lane, node-tree's root fans,
  reading-flow's wraps. About 5 of the +15 are this. The rest are real detours
  (vn-architecture, hn-offline v0, code-layers v0).
- **`uneven-padding` and `padding-varies` are real but misattributed.** Each hit is a channel
  that `channels.ts` reserved and the router didn't use, as in file-session's Node server left
  56px and mcp-call's MCP server right 40px. The script exempts padding that carries a line,
  as it should. The defect is the unused channel, not the padding rule.
- **`group-staircase` at the root fires only on a monotonic step.** hn-offline v0's Phone
  browser (top right), server (mid-left) and Upstream (bottom right) don't step monotonically,
  so the metric is silent, while the top-left quarter of the drawing is empty.

MISSED kinds with two or more lines (each needs a metric next):

- **Empty quarters at the root** (group-packing, 4 lines: hn-offline v0, scan v0/e1,
  vn-architecture v0's Services). `empty-band` only counts full-width or full-height gaps. A
  metric for the largest empty rectangle inside the drawing's hull, as a share of its area,
  would catch these.
- **Diamond entries and exits off the centre line** (routing-ports, 4 lines: reading-flow v0,
  e1, e2 and coolify v0). `off-axis-port` skips diamonds.
- **Side exits in trees** (routing-ports, 3 lines: node-tree e1/e2, annotations e1).
- **Long strips** (engine-spacing, 2 lines: file-session e2 at 9:1, ufw e1 at 7:1). There is no
  defect for aspect, only the recorded ratio. A defect over 5:1 would catch them.

## What changed, and which product decisions cost too much

Each round-4 decision, with what it cost in this audit:

1. **Theme switch holds rows and order (#64): kept.** Release e2 and annotations e1 pass. The
   cost: ragged row tops in a taller theme (S3), and gaps too narrow for relabel pockets (#65's
   note on add-metric e2).
2. **Relabel widens only the gap that needs it (#65): kept, but the expectation is wrong.** All
   four relabel `still *` checks fail by construction (pgm e2, add-metric e2, ingest e1,
   coolify e1). Moves are 20–87px, and only add-metric flips order. The rubric should check
   relabels with `keeps-order` plus "objects off the widened band hold still". With `still *`,
   the failure marks a policy, not a defect.
3. **Growth promises keeps-order (#68): kept.** One of four held-out growth checks newly
   passes (regionshot). The remaining failures are engine sweeps (v2ctx, ufw, tasks) and stale
   viewer stays (hn-offline e2).
4. **Channels may cost ≤ 10% area (#66): the area cost is fine, the waste is not.** Channels
   are reserved before routing. Where the router then takes another way, the group keeps an
   empty, lopsided margin: uneven-padding 0 → 4 corpus, 0 → 1 held-out, 0 → 2 examples.
5. **Lines never cross title bands (#66): cost looks too high in a few drawings.** Lines that
   used to slip through a band now go round the whole group:
   - web-app's useSession → ProfileCard: ~2,000px
   - vn-architecture e2's query / action: over the whole drawing and back
   - vn-architecture's long-edges: 0 → 2/4/2
     The band rule probably drives most of the +15 long-edge and +2 crossings. A capped
     exception would remove the worst cases: allow a band crossing straight down into a member
     when the detour is over 3 × the direct length.
6. **Returns take the shorter side (#67): cost too high in one case, which is S1.** The lane
   lands in the target's facing gap even when that gap holds the forward edge's label (release
   v0–e2). The rule needs a second test: a lane may not arrive through a gap whose label it
   would cross. In that case it takes the other side, or arrives on the target's side face.
7. **Groups pack tight (#63): kept, two costs.**
   - Gaps between chain members don't widen for the lines that leave between them. The
     file-session 40px gap carries three lines and a label.
   - Straight chains turn some drawings into 7–9:1 strips: file-session e2, ufw e1,
     order-pipeline.
   - hn-offline e2 is the known third cost: stale viewer stays.
8. **Bundles keep a 16px stagger (#60, #69): kept, no cost seen.** The ragged-bends hits that
   remain (11) are fans where one edge takes a different corridor.

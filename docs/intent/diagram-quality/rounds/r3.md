## The user's three standing concerns

- **Edges not hugging boxes or borders.** Hugs 223 → 183 and border runs 82 → 45 (#53, #55). What
  is left is mostly lines with nowhere else to go: a route that must pass a group passes just
  outside its border (hn-deploy, scan, vn-architecture), a return inside a group runs in the slot
  between its members and its border (file-session `redraw`, vn-architecture `tool call`), and
  loops round a tall column ride the column's side (release `wait for more`, decisory `save`).
- **Routing that looks drafted.** Hooks halved (72 → 31) and off-axis ports fell 51 → 21 (#51,
  #54); downward trees and layered flows read like a draftsman's again (node-tree back to 4.5).
  Orderliness got slightly worse in count: ragged fans 15 → 20, lopsided ports 6 → 8, jogs 0 → 5
  (now 1 after #58), many-bends 41 → 50 as lines detour for clearance. Fans still use three sides
  of a box when one would do (pgm server, tasks `/api/trpc handler`).
- **Containers fit their children evenly.** Padding is even and no resize cycles were seen. What a
  reader notices is the layout inside and between groups: compound-flow staircases (hn-offline,
  tasks, ufw), groups far bigger than their members (tasks `Next.js server` and `Data`, web-app),
  and sibling groups pressed 8px apart (ufw e1). None of this has a metric yet.

## False alarms and metric notes

- `left-of build runtime` (hn-deploy) and `above … ` on a stress layout (code-layers) remain known
  false alarms; 1px and 9px `near` misses (decisory, add-metric, scan) are not defects.
- Twelve of 20 `ragged-bends` are bundles entering one side at one edge-gap's stagger, which a
  draftsman would draw that way. The metric should allow turns that step by the edge gap.
- `mid-word-wrap` (new, #56) was MISSED in nine renders before it existed.

MISSED kinds with two or more lines (each needs a metric next):

- **Group interior holes, staircases and groups pressed together** (group-packing, 5 lines).
- **Two labels crowding one short gap or reading as one edge** (label-placement, 4 lines).
- **A side branch folded away from its source** (engine-order: ci-checks Upload screenshots,
  v2ctx Contact sheet).
- **Lines riding inside a group between its members and its border** (routing-clearance, 3 lines;
  `edge-near-group-border` counts runs outside and inside but not a run that is also near a box).

## Fixes this round

Chosen from the ranking below as the causes fixable now; each is its own PR, stacked.

1. **#56 Keep node titles from breaking inside a word** (text-wrap). Long words break where an
   identifier's parts meet; a default-sized node grows up to twice its width to keep a word
   whole. New `mid-word-wrap` metric. Corpus mid-word-wrap 11 → 0, held-out 15 → 0.
2. **#57 Keep a flow's fold when edges are labelled** (stability). The fold is planned on the
   run without labels; the labelled run keeps the band count within a quarter of fitting.
   ingest e1 320px mean move / 17 flips → 26px / 0 flips; coolify e1 4 rows → 3.
3. **#58 Straighten small steps in routes** (routing-ports / orderliness). A tidy step slides one
   of two parallel runs onto the other's line, through fold-lane waypoints. Jogs 5 → 1 (held-out
   1 → 0).

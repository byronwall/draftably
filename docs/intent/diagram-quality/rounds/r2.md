## The user's three standing concerns

- **Edges too close to boxes.** Much better between boxes (hugs 436 → 223), but the remaining
  cases moved to group borders: a line running 10–15px inside or outside a group's border, or
  squeezed into the 24px slot between a group border and its boxes (file-session redraw,
  vn-architecture tool call, hn-deploy, hexagonal-billing, kubernetes, multi-region). The
  `edge-on-group-border` metric only counts runs under 8px, so most of these are MISSED.
- **Routing that looks drafted.** Fan-outs from one side now share a turn line and ports are
  centred, but returns, wraps and far-side entries are drawn as one-off loops: hooks rose 66 → 72
  and crossings 29 → 40. The worst offenders are fold wraps (ci-checks, data-lakehouse, ci-cd,
  hn-deploy), each wrap edge routed as its own loop instead of sharing one return channel.
- **Containers fit their children.** Solved for padding (uneven-padding 21 → 0, no resize
  cycles seen). What's left is layout inside the group: compound-flow staircases
  (hn-offline, ufw), staggered members (vn-architecture clients), and interior holes in big
  nested groups (web-app-components).

## Metric changes this round

`scripts/diagram-audit.mjs` (also in [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md)); both rounds were measured with the
changed script.

- **Fold wraps are not back edges or long edges.** An edge into a later row of a folded flow reads
  forward (as `reads-x` already treats it). It was counted as a back edge, which failed
  regionshot's `back-edges` on the wrap alone, and as a long edge on every fold.
- **`uneven-ports` ignores ports on a diamond's slanted faces.** They fell through to "bottom" and
  produced 9 of round 1's lopsided-port reports (orderliness worker's note).
- **New `off-axis-port`** (weight 1): with a declared `direction`, a forward edge that arrives
  sideways or leaves against the direction. It catches the node-tree and exploreda regression,
  which no other metric saw.
- Held-out authoring fix before scoring: regionshot v0 declares three return edges, so its
  expectation is `back-edges 3`, not 2.

## Root causes, ranked for round 3

1. **Folds cut in the wrong place and draw their wraps as loose loops** (engine-order +
   routing-detour: 17 S2 lines across ci-checks, data-lakehouse, ci-cd, decisory, reading-flow,
   add-metric, hn-deploy, ingest, order-lifecycle, support-triage; both worse examples).
   `foldRanks` in [src/dsl/compile.ts](../../../../src/dsl/compile.ts) (line 274) picks a cut by
   the number of edges crossing it plus distance from an even share, so it will cut between a
   decision and its own branch, or inside a loop. The wrap edges are then routed one by one, each
   taking its own path around the bands. Fix: never cut inside a branch or loop (cut only where
   the main chain alone crosses), keep a decision with its branches, and route all wraps across
   one cut through a shared channel in the band gap.
2. **Downward layouts enter children from the side** (routing-ports regression from #40:
   node-tree 4.5 → 3.5, exploreda worse, ci-checks e2; off-axis-port 51). The router's candidate
   `score()` in [src/engines/routing-engine.js](../../../../src/engines/routing-engine.js) (line 656) penalises a stub pointing away from the target but knows nothing of the layout direction,
   and `standoffCost` (line 200) makes the two-bend bottom → mid-gap → top route cost more than a
   one-bend L into the child's side. Fix: pass the layout's direction to the router and make the
   rank-facing sides the default for forward edges (top for down, left for right), costing more
   than the standoff.
3. **Lines ride group borders** (routing-clearance: 26 lines, 13 diagrams, 10 MISSED). The
   standoff is halved on borders (`band: s.standoff / 2`, routing-engine.js line 514) and group
   padding is 24px, so a line entering or leaving a group runs 10–15px from its border or in the
   slot between border and boxes. Fix: full standoff on borders, and when a route must run inside
   a group, give it the middle of the gap rather than the border side. Raise the metric to 15px.
4. **Far-side hooks and fans drawn from four sides** (routing-detour + routing-order: hooks
   66 → 72, crossings 29 → 40; pgm, scan, add-metric, vn-architecture). When a facing side
   already has a port, `fanoutPenalty` and `portStability` in the same `score()` push the next
   edge to a far side and around the target. Fix: allow a second and third port on the facing
   side (spaced by `port-gap`) before any far side, and route a node's fan to several targets
   from one side.
5. **Relabels and theme switches move the drawing** (stability + theme-metrics: pgm e2,
   add-metric e2, ingest e1 still * failures; release e2). Label-aware gaps (`roomBetween`,
   compile.ts line 173; fold `bandGap`, line 314) re-run the layout, and a fold can gain a row.
   Fix: keep the fold count and positions from before when only labels change, widening just the
   gaps that need it; measure text so the theme font doesn't change wraps. Product question for
   the user: should a relabel keep `still *`, or is `keeps-order` the right promise now that gaps
   grow to fit labels?

Next in line: compound-flow staircases and staggered group members (group-packing), mid-word

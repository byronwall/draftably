## Method and interpretation

The standing FEEL-based judge guide was used without changes. Each complete pair was inspected for reading order, connection identity, label ownership, group boundaries and local spacing. Existing SVGs were rasterized or cropped with the unchanged repository screenshot script when the fitted overview could not resolve detail. Producing a crop was followed by an actual image view. The PNG dimensions and SHA-256 hashes of every inspected pair and crop are recorded in the JSON.

The two cards scale their SVGs independently. No physical drawing-area claim is made from their displayed size. A long drawing can still incur an overview-legibility cost, but tiny fitted text was not automatically treated as intrinsically unreadable. Exact requested presentation dimensions were absent from the public manifest, so P046 and P058 do not establish format compliance. P047 supplies no prior authored state, so its appearance cannot establish pin or scope preservation.

Pair severity measures the difference between the two candidates. A tied pair may contain a serious defect common to both; the separate defect ledger retains those defects. Confidence is a subjective judgment confidence, not a calibrated statistical probability.

## What the close inspections established

The strongest differences involve connection or content identity. In P045, A’s regular-looking row conceals horizontal connectors behind successive component boxes. B remains a difficult nested drawing, but avoids that specific misleading chain appearance. In P048, B’s decode connection visibly strikes the packet header’s bit-index row; A preserves those specialized annotations, despite placing the queue farther from its related workers. These are severity-3 visual differences, without a claim about any programmatic hard gate.

A common label-sizing failure appears in P046 and P058: Stage 36’s long title extends above and below its box into connector space. It is kept as a severity-3 defect in both panels, including the tied P046 judgment. P058’s A preference concerns its clearer whole-process rows, not a claim that its label failure is acceptable.

Details also rejected false alarms. P040’s bottom subsystem is outside the main system boundary in both panels; an apparent difference in boundary ownership at overview scale was not retained. P052’s State 40 has its normal pill outline in both panels when viewed closely. The P047 local crops have identical file hashes, supporting a visual tie without inferring the hidden candidate identities.

P052, P053, P054 and P056 favor the more even, nearby repeat branches only at severity 1. These repeated lifecycle comparisons are polishing evidence, not four independent major coverage wins. P041 remains tied because its vertical-versus-horizontal change did not impair the stated task at inspected detail scale, and no preferred orientation was given.

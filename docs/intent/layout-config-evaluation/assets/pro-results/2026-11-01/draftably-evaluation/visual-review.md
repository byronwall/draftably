# Visual review and metric disagreements

**Measured:** 63 matched production-render pairs were inspected under hidden candidate identities. 24 favored A, 18 favored B, and 21 were ties. Judgments were frozen before the keys were read.

The reviewer was one expert model, not independent human validation. Every required PNG was opened. Detail renders are documented in the phase narratives. Several pairs reuse a graph to isolate a control, so pair counts are not counts of independent graph discoveries or users.

## Rendering and blinding

All sheets use the shipped production SVG renderer, font metrics, routing and label placement, then the unchanged repository screenshot tool. A deterministic SHA-256 shuffle (seed `blind-20261101-<pair>`) places candidates left/right. Public manifests omit method identities; separate key files disclose them after review. Missing or timed-out full routes cannot be manufactured into a complete comparison.

The first 36 pairs were reviewed before policy sealing. Twenty-four fresh heldout pairs were reviewed after sealing, followed in this ledger by three additional archived-development niche pairs. Those three reviews are qualitative evidence only and did not tune any policy or band.

Each pair panel fits its own SVG to the card. Whole-diagram judgments address structure, tracing and legibility in that presentation; they are not direct measurements of absolute physical area. The saved bounding-box area and route length remain the quantitative references. Detail crops are listed in the phase narratives.

## Agreement with diagnostic weights

The three-way prediction prefers the hard-valid output when validity differs. Otherwise it uses the selected DI weighting with a one-unit tie band. This is not the full profile-oracle acceptance rule, which also checks area and route length.

| Weighting | Same three-way verdict | Numeric preference / visual tie | Numeric tie / visual preference | Opposite preference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| balanced | 39/63 | 9 | 6 | 9 |
| traceable | 39/63 | 9 | 6 | 9 |
| direct | 41/63 | 10 | 4 | 8 |

The symmetric development-style band (balanced score within 1, area within 10%, route length within 10%, both hard-valid) contains 10 reviewed pairs. Visual preferences inside it: P017. This is a targeted calibration check, not an empirical claim that all changes inside the band are unnoticeable. The profile-dependent oracle rule remains separately specified in evaluation-method.md.

The numerical weights deliberately remain frozen. The ledger below keeps disagreement evidence rather than silently adjusting heldout scores. Honest crossings can be preferable to long detours; corner rounding can matter in appearance while the polyline is identical; a concise path can still detach its label from its owner.

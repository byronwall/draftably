## Why accepted

The narrow central stack provides orientation while large green and blue loops communicate two different cycles and the value of shorter returns. Restrained colors, generous white space, small role icons, and aligned terminals handle considerable complexity.

Intended reading path: Top resource inputs feed the central manufacturer/service stack. Biological paths return through left-hand systems; technical loops on the right return to successively earlier stages. Bottom leakage is a common escape.

Patterns: industrial material flow, nested feedback loops, bilateral comparison, flow hierarchy. Principal objects: **22**. Twenty-two labeled material, service, use, recovery, and leakage roles represented natively; repeated icons and return arcs are additional graphic marks.

Confirmation case: **no**. Part of the development/evaluation set.

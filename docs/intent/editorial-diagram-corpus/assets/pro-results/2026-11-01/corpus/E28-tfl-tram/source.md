## Why accepted

Consistent octilinear geometry, clear green route identities, alternating exterior labels, a careful Croydon loop, and terse interchange symbols make a large network readable at modest publication size.

Intended reading path: Follow the west-to-east Wimbledon line into the one-way Croydon loop, then distinguish branches to Beckenham Junction, Elmers End, and New Addington. Interchange symbols qualify named stops.

Patterns: transport map, branching network, one-way cycle, external labels, dense stress case. Principal objects: **39**. Thirty-nine named stops. Route colors, interchange symbols, stop ticks, direction arrows, and the key are additional semantic marks.

Confirmation case: **yes**. Audited now; reserved from numerical tuning recommendations for future changes.

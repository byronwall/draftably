## Why accepted

The double-loop composition makes different stellar timescales and outcomes visible at once. Varied celestial silhouettes, scale, pale cyan labels, and generous black space provide hierarchy without boxes.

Intended reading path: From the shared central nebula, follow the lower-mass left loop or higher-mass right loop; the latter splits into two remnants. Return arrows connect expelled material to the nebula.

Patterns: scientific lifecycle, branching, circular feedback, dark composition. Principal objects: **12**. Twelve principal stages/illustrated objects, counting the two mass-dependent protostar branches separately in the native model.

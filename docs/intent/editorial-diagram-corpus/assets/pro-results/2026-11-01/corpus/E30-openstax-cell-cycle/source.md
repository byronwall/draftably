## Why accepted

A nearly complete radial band makes interphase visually dominant, while a small peach mitotic sector and outer bracket separate the short division stage. Color follows phase membership, and a concise daughter-cell outcome label anchors the biological result.

Intended reading path: Follow the circular red arrows through G₁, S, G₂, mitosis, and cytokinesis. Read the outer Interphase and Mitotic phase bands as membership/extent; cytokinesis produces two daughter cells.

Patterns: scientific cycle, radial bands, phase containment, sector annotation. Principal objects: **6**. Five named phases plus one text outcome, Formation of 2 daughter cells. The two phase-group labels are not counted as principal objects. Native attempts use the same six nodes plus two groups.

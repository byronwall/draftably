## Why accepted

A pale terrain block allows saturated blue water to stand out. Geographic position, depth, repeated directional flux marks, italic process labels, and compact explanatory prose integrate natural and human systems.

Intended reading path: Read the atmospheric band and precipitation, scan the mountain-to-ocean landscape, then the subsurface cutaway. The legend and footer explain storage, movement, human use, water quality, and climate.

Patterns: scientific physical system, geographic cutaway, labeled fluxes, dense stress case, human/natural comparison. Principal objects: **20**. Twenty named water pools and human-use sites represented as native nodes. Terrain, weather, landscape structures, and repeated flux marks add many visual objects beyond this deliberately limited principal count.

Confirmation case: **no**. Part of the development/evaluation set.

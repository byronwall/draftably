Eight independent publishers supply eight adjacent references: NASA Science, NOAA Climate.gov, USGS, Design Council, Ellen MacArthur Foundation, Transport for London, COPE and OpenStax. All eight full comparison sheets used supplied `shoot --sheet`. Every original, v0, v1 and final v2 was visually inspected. Detail crop selections are recorded in all eight `detail-selection.json` files for the root's batch supplied-shoot pass.

The source snapshot was not edited. Native sources, actual renders, geometry, SHA-256 freeze records, validation records and subprocess logs are present. No source image was imported into a native node, no tracing/overlay renderer was used, and no custom screenshot program replaced the supplied script.

## What the first renders tell us

E26's default two-group four-stage process is a clean diagram on its own merits. It is comparable for reading phase order, while losing the source's divergence/convergence mechanism. E30's default grouped cell-cycle sequence is also clear and easier to maintain than its more literal manual attempt. These are useful default strengths; a lower fidelity score does not negate them.

The default becomes substantially less effective when the source relies on distributed physical space, nested curved returns, or station points distinct from exterior labels. E25 has 30 measured crossings and no landscape cutaway. E27 has 19 crossings and loses its bilateral loop hierarchy. E28 turns an almost horizontal transit map into a tall station list. The latter used a layered recipe even though native documentation explicitly says undirected connections do not impose flow ranks; do not report this chosen-recipe consequence as a proven ranking bug.

E24 demonstrates useful native headroom. Structural styling alone preserves its logic, but manual sizes, ports and pins are needed to recover the editorial stair. The final chart has zero crossings and five bends. E23 improves from two crossings to zero and becomes a useful paired lifecycle diagram. Neither case needs a new general layout engine to be useful.

## Avoid misleading conclusions

- A missing native keyword does not prove the internal model lacks the feature. Outside label modes and icon/image styles exist internally; native serialization/round-trip support is the boundary this study exercised.
- Fewer crossings are not semantic completeness. E25 drops from 30 to 7, and E27 from 19 to 4, while their physical and nested-flow mechanisms remain reduced.
- Arbitrary per-edge colors would help E27/E28, but a passenger map also needs service continuation, shared strokes, stop marks, external labels and controlled geometry.
- E25 is explicitly a generalized graph projection. It does not preserve every local water-supply/recharge/evaporation relationship. Its semantic rating remains 2 in every stage.
- E26's short final source is not a cost win without qualification: it merges four independent phases into two text strings.
- E30 has a text daughter-cell outcome, not daughter-cell illustrations. Its final soft-split diagnostic is visible and its quality score is lower than the clear default.
- Small type in the first manual NASA/NOAA drafts was mainly an authoring decision. A final spacing pass improved it, and the initial valid v2 sources/renders remain in `trials/`. Absence of text-scale controls makes authoring harder; it does not make all chosen whitespace a renderer bug.
- Native sources remain reviewable. No claim of pixel perfection, exact visual-difference error, measured authoring time or tested label-edit resilience is made.

## Iteration and remaining warnings

E23 and E24 each use three cycles after v0: structural v1, initial manual v2, and a final spacing compaction. Both earlier manual v2s are retained with actual renders and logs. E26 uses three cycles: v1, an invalid initial v2 (`gap=0` outside the documented range), and a corrected final v2. This was an authoring error, not an application failure. All other adjacent cases use two cycles after v0.

Final execution warnings: E24 has four text-overflow warnings for compact diamonds; E30 has the soft-split information note, counted as one warning in metrics. E25/E27/E29 retain measured crossings despite successful non-metrics `check`; the geometry metrics are reported separately. No final native command failed. The failed initial E26 commands and failed sheet are preserved in logs rather than removed.

No local renderer work is left unexecuted for these eight cases. The root's supplemental corpus audit and detail crops may add further evidence. Future source changes, implementation work, permission review before publishing third-party media, and any repository PR remain outside these native trials.

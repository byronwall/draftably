Routing

One routing statement sets the router for every connection:

Copy
routing edge-gap=24 clearance=12 corner-radius=0

Settings: clearance, standoff, port-gap, edge-gap, bend-penalty, crossing-penalty, shared-penalty, parallel-penalty, port-stability, fanout-penalty, passes and corner-radius, with the router's own ranges. Without it, the router's defaults apply.

clearance (default 10) is the hard distance a line keeps from boxes. standoff (default 20, 0 to 40) is the distance the router prefers for a line running alongside a box or a group border, inside or outside it. A line comes closer only when no clearer route exists. Only a group's title blocks its header band, so a line can enter a group straight down beside the title. standoff=0 turns the preference off.

When the root layout line declares direction=right or direction=down, a forward connection between objects that layout places leaves its source's trailing side and enters its target's leading side (bottom to top going down), turning in the gap between ranks. It uses another side only when those are blocked. Connections inside a group with its own recipe are left to that recipe.

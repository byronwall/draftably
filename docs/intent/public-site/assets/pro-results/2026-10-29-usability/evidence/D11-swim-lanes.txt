Each step takes its rank from the connections of the whole drawing, so one rank is one column across every lane, and a step that follows another sits after it even when it is in a different lane. Steps of one lane in one rank stack across it. With direction=right (the default) lanes are bands stacked top to bottom, in file order or the order an order root line gives; with direction=down they are columns side by side and the steps run down the page. Every lane is as long as the drawing. gap= sets the least gap between ranks (56 at least).

A group inside a lane follows its own frame=: a boxed group takes one place, and a soft group's members rank on their own. Position refinements inside a lane (near, stay, nudge, pin) do not apply yet. Lines that run back against the order take the long way round the lanes.

Sequence diagrams now draw what the file says. Root and group `order` both apply. A phase must be a run of consecutive messages, a message must end on a participant, and a band needs a participant. Distant labels and self-messages get their full room, and plain headers get a visible outline. Deleting a message or participant cleans its phase, and a drag saves a lane order. Changing the theme keeps the subtitle, notes, legend and states. In any diagram, the editor can now delete a selected connection while the Inspector is open.

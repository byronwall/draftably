A label on a mind-map branch or an org-chart reporting line no longer takes that line out of the tree. The line keeps its place and draws its label between the columns, so the topics or people under it stay in the chart. A label never changes which lines form the tree: write a dotted-line report or a cross-link with `kind=async`.

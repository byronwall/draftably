Put icons in boxes: `icon=database` draws a Lucide icon before a node's label, `icon-pos=top` draws it as a large tile over the label, and `icon=` also works on a group's title band and a legend entry. `prefix:name` (`logos:aws-lambda`) reads any installed `@iconify-json/<prefix>` set. One-color icons follow the theme's text color. `draftably icons <word>` finds names, and `check` suggests a close name for an unknown one.

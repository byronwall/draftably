---
'draftably': patch
---

Flow edges leave boxes on the right or bottom and enter on the left or top in a directed layout. Lines into members of a group that another line targets are no longer drawn along the member's border. A line to a group meets the group's side near its own line. A line out of a group crosses the title band when the way round reads backwards. `check` warns when a line has to leave or enter by a backward side.

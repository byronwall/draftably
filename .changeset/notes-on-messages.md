A sequence `note` can now anchor to a message with `near=<message id>`. `side=right` (the default) and `side=left` place it in the margin level with the row, and the panel grows round it. `side=above` and `side=below` look for a clear spot along the row. A note never moves a lane or a row. Deleting a message or participant keeps the note's text and moves it under the subtitle.

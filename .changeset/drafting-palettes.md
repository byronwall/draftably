New theme palettes. `drafting` is the new default: navy ink on cool paper with drafting blue and marker gold. `ink` replaces `mulberry`, `blueprint` replaces `midnight`, `fieldnotes` now pairs indigo with terracotta, and `signal` uses Okabe–Ito's color-blind-safe hues. Rename `theme graphite`, `theme mulberry` and `theme midnight` lines to `drafting`, `ink` and `blueprint`. Amber and orange stay bright with dark text at shades 400–500, and change marks differ in lightness so they read for red-green color blindness.

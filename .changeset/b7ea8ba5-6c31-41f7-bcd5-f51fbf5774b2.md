'@draftably/runtime': minor
---

Add `@draftably/runtime/graph`: lay out and route a graph your app draws. `layout()` takes plain nodes, groups and edges with any IDs, and returns each box's position and size, each edge's routed line and label box, and the equal `.draft` file. `route()` routes lines between boxes your app placed. `routing.quality` chooses between full and quick routing; `auto` keeps large graphs near a second.

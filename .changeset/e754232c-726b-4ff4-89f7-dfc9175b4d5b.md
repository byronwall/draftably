A long group title wraps to the content width when no word would break, so the frame no longer widens to 240 px around a narrow column. A folded flow puts sibling branches that wrap into the next row last, so their wrap lines cross no sibling edge. draftably vary offers compound-flow for a grouped spring or grid root, and ranks a variant first when it removes most bends at a similar width or is much smaller with shorter lines.

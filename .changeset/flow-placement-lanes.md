`flow` lines each object up with what it connects to in the rank before, and widens a gap between ranks until each line that turns in it has its own lane. Trees and dense graphs draw with far fewer crossings and bends, and route several times faster: a 63-node tree has no crossings and lays out in about 0.3 s instead of 5 s. The router's grid search is also about a third faster, with the same routes.

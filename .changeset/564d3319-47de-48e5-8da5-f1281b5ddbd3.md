---
'draftably': minor
---

New `draftably pdf <files|folder> -o out.pdf` writes one vector PDF of a diagram set: a page per diagram (or per state with `--states`) with its heading and key, `--page fit|letter|a4|slide`, `--order given|name|title`, a cover and contents with `--title`, bookmarks, and each file's source attached. `check`, `extract` and `open` read a PDF's sources back (`out.pdf#name.draft` names one). The viewer has Export PDF… in the export menu and a PDF button in the files panel to pick, order and export several diagrams.

`layout` and `route` from `@draftably/runtime/graph` take React Flow and Svelte Flow graphs under `reactFlow` and `svelteFlow`, and return them in the same shape: nodes with positions relative to their group and sizes, edges with routed paths and label boxes in `data.draftably`. `route` routes the lines again where the nodes are now, so lines follow a dragged node.

Route lines faster, with the same routes as before. The router skips line pairs that are too far apart to meet, compares segments as plain numbers, scores a candidate against other lines only while it can still win, and counts totals once per pass. A 100-node chain routes about 7 times faster, a 60-node tree and a 100-node DAG about 5 times faster, and a node with 16 children about 2.4 times faster.

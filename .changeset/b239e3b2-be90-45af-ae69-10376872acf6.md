Route lines faster. The router skips line pairs that are too far apart to cross or crowd each other, scores a candidate route against other lines only when its own length and sides can still win, and counts totals once per routing pass. Routes stay the same; a 100-node chain routes about 7 times faster and a 60-node tree about 3 times faster.

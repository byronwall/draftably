`color=amber` draws a clear warm yellow instead of ochre. Amber 400 and 500 are lighter and keep their full color, and the pale amber shades are a little lighter to make room. Text on amber stays above 8:1 contrast. Orange, red, stone and the other families do not change. A theme's gold `secondary` (Signal, Blueprint) keeps its anchor shade, and its other shades follow the same lighter amber ramp.

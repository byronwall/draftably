Draw database tables: a group body lists its columns, `layout g table` draws keys and types, and `-> table.column` draws a crow's-foot line. Adds the line ends `one`, `many`, `zero-or-one`, `zero-or-many` and `one-or-many`, and `head=`/`tail=` on `legend line`.

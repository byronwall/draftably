The viewer copies a diagram to the clipboard: Copy image (⇧⌘C, the export menu and the canvas right-click menu) writes PNG, SVG, HTML and SVG text in one copy, falling back to what the browser accepts, and the toast names the formats. With something selected it copies only the selection. Copy as gives SVG markup, the .draft source or a link. One export dialog for PNG, SVG, PDF and Copy shows a preview and remembers scale, background and heading. `inspect --clipboard` puts a PNG and the SVG text on the macOS clipboard.

Nested groups of one color family are drawn in stepped shades (100, 200, 300; 900, 800, 700 in Midnight) instead of one repeated fill, and a node whose shade is within two steps of its own-family container moves three steps past it. `check` notes more than four accent families (`many-colors`) and members that repeat their container's family (`color-matches-container`). The language guide and the agent skill give concrete color rules: two to four accent families with a role each, no red-next-to-green, color the outer group and let members inherit.

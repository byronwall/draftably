`check` warns (`[relaxed]`) when a `near`, `stay` or `nudge` gives way by 16 px or more, instead of an `info` note. `format --drop-stale` removes those refinements and stays the arrangement outgrew. `size id width=… height=auto` fits the title's height at that width, so a size line survives label edits.

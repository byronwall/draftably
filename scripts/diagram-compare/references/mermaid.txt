Condensed from the Mermaid flowchart and state diagram docs (https://mermaid.js.org/syntax/flowchart.html).

A flowchart starts with `flowchart` and a direction: TB or TD (top to bottom), BT, LR, RL.

flowchart LR
    id1[Rectangle] --> id2(Rounded)
    id2 --> id3{Decision}
    id3 -->|yes| id4([Stadium])
    id3 -- no --> id5[(Database)]
    id4 -.-> id6((Circle))

Node shapes: id[text] rectangle, id(text) rounded, id([text]) stadium, id[[text]] subroutine,
id[(text)] cylinder, id((text)) circle, id{text} rhombus, id{{text}} hexagon.
Put text with special characters in quotes: id["Text (with) symbols"].

Links: A --> B arrow, A --- B open line, A -.-> B dotted, A ==> B thick.
Text on a link: A -->|text| B or A -- text --> B.

Subgraphs group nodes:

subgraph one [Group title]
    a1 --> a2
end
subgraph two [Other group]
    direction TB
    b1 --> b2
end
a2 --> b1

A link can start or end at a subgraph id. `title` front matter sets a title:

---
title: My diagram
---
flowchart TD

State diagrams: `stateDiagram-v2`, then `[*] --> Still`, `Still --> Moving : push`, `Moving --> [*]`.
Comments start with %%.

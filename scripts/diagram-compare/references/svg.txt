The root needs xmlns. viewBox sets the user coordinate system; x grows right, y grows down.
Basic shapes: rect (x, y, width, height, rx), circle (cx, cy, r), ellipse, line (x1, y1, x2, y2),
polyline and polygon (points), path (d).
Path commands: M move, L line, H and V horizontal and vertical lines, C cubic curve,
Q quadratic curve, A arc, Z close. Lowercase letters use relative coordinates.
text draws at (x, y) on its baseline; text-anchor start|middle|end aligns it.
Group with <g>, and move a group with transform="translate(x,y)".
fill, stroke and stroke-width style shapes; stroke-dasharray draws dashes.
A marker in <defs> puts arrowheads on line ends with marker-end.
Later elements paint over earlier ones.
